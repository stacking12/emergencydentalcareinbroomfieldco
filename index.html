Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, swelling in the face or gums, a dental abscess, uncontrolled bleeding and mouth injuries all count. If you're unsure, call us and we will help you decide.

Can I get same day emergency dental care in Broomfield?

Yes. We prioritize urgent cases and offer same day appointments for emergency dental care whenever possible. Call 303-763-1953 as early as you can.

Are you open after hours?

We provide 24 hour and after hours dentist support for urgent problems. Call us and we will guide you on the quickest way to be seen.

What should I do if a tooth gets knocked out?

Pick it up by the crown, not the root. Rinse it gently with water without scrubbing, and try to place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Then call us immediately; the sooner you're seen, the better the chance of saving it.

How much does an emergency dentist visit cost?

Cost depends on the exam and treatment needed. We keep our emergency care affordable and will explain the price before we begin. Call us for details on payment options.
